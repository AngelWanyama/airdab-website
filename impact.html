Our 2035 contribution

Stronger rural enterprises, empowered women and youth, improved agricultural livelihoods, restored ecosystems and sustainable management of natural resources.

Our strategy recognizes that rural poverty, climate change, unemployment, food insecurity and biodiversity loss are interconnected challenges. We therefore pursue integrated solutions that improve livelihoods while protecting and restoring the natural resources upon which rural communities depend.
