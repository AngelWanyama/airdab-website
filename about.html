Support Us

Rural development with nature at the center.

We were established to contribute to sustainable rural development and biodiversity conservation through community-centered interventions.

Our story

Connected challenges need integrated solutions.

Rural poverty, environmental degradation and climate vulnerability are closely interconnected. Our programming approach recognizes rural communities as active decision-makers, implementers and custodians of natural resources.

We work with communities, farmer groups, cooperatives, youth groups, women-led enterprises, local governments, private-sector actors, government ministries and agencies, research institutions and development partners.

Our journey

From grassroots initiative to registered institution.

2018

Foundation

AIRDAB begins working directly with rural communities in Kasese, focused on climate-smart agriculture, biodiversity conservation and rural livelihoods.

2025

Formally incorporated

AIRDAB is registered with the Uganda Registration Services Bureau as a Company Limited by Guarantee, formalising our accountability as an independent not-for-profit.

Today

Eight programs, six services

Based in Kasese, Uganda, delivering across eight program areas and six services with communities, government, private-sector and research partners.

Our vision

Resilient rural communities living sustainably in harmony with nature and supported by inclusive and thriving rural economies.

Our mission

To improve the livelihoods and resilience of rural communities through sustainable agriculture, inclusive enterprise development, climate action, biodiversity conservation, ecosystem restoration, knowledge and strategic partnerships.
