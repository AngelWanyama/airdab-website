FAQ

Your Questions Answered

How does AIRDAB empower youth and women?

AIRDAB empowers rural youth, women and refugees by providing them with the skills and resources needed to start and grow their own businesses, fostering economic independence and community development.

What are AIRDAB's biodiversity goals?

Our biodiversity conservation efforts focus on protecting natural habitats and endangered species through community-led initiatives, sustainable resource management, and educational programs.

Who does AIRDAB partner with?

We partner with local communities, governments, and international organizations to maximize our impact and create lasting positive change for both people and the planet.

How is AIRDAB's success measured?

Success is measured by the increased income and improved livelihoods of the communities we serve, as well as the measurable improvements in biodiversity indicators within our project areas.

How can I support AIRDAB?

Individuals can support AIRDAB by donating, volunteering their time and skills, or partnering with us on specific projects. Every contribution makes a difference!
