Development and conservation belong in the same conversation.

We work at the intersection of rural economic development, climate resilience, agriculture, biodiversity conservation, youth and women empowerment, enterprise development, and community-based natural resource management.

Climate resilience

Stronger community and institutional capacity to manage water and respond to climate-related risks.

Rural livelihoods

Sustainable and climate-smart agriculture that strengthens productivity, food security, incomes and resilience.

Biodiversity conservation

Restoration and sustainable management of ecosystems and natural resources with community participation.

Youth and women

Skills, entrepreneurship, finance, markets and employment opportunities for youth, women and vulnerable groups.
