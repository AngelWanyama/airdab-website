Cookie Policy.

What this website stores in your browser and why. In short: no tracking, no advertising and no analytics.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's "local storage", work in the same way. Websites use them to work properly, to remember choices, or to track visitors.

2. What this website uses

The AIRDAB website does not set any cookies. It uses no analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking tools. It saves one item in your browser's local storage, and only after you send a form:

Items this website stores in your browser
NameTypePurposeHow long it is kept
airdab-form-sends Local storage (strictly necessary) Records when you last sent our Contact or Support Us form, so that the website can block spam by allowing no more than 3 messages in 10 minutes. It contains only times, with no name or other personal details. Only times from the last 10 minutes are used. The item stays in your browser until you clear your browsing data.

This item is strictly necessary to protect the website's forms from abuse, so it does not need your consent. Our fonts are stored on our own server, so loading a page sends no information to font providers.

3. Google Maps on the Contact page

The map on our Contact page is provided by Google. It does not load until you press "Show map". When you do, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, for example to remember preferences or to protect its services. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab). If you prefer, you can use the "Get directions" link or our written address instead.

4. Why there is no cookie banner

A consent banner is needed when a website uses cookies that are not strictly necessary, such as analytics or advertising cookies. This website does not. The only third-party service that could set cookies, Google Maps, waits until you choose to load it.

5. Staff Webmail

The "Staff Webmail" link in the footer is for AIRDAB staff only. The webmail login page uses cookies that are needed to sign in and keep the session secure.

6. How to control or delete them

You can view and delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings, usually under "Privacy" or "Clear browsing data". Clearing them will not stop this website from working.

7. Changes and contact

If we add any cookie or similar technology that is not strictly necessary, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before using it. Questions? Email airdabuganda@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
