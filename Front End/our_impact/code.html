Case Study 01 — Education

The Jakarta Literacy Initiative

Addressing systemic educational gaps in urban Jakarta required more than just building schools. We implemented a holistic, community-led curriculum that integrated digital literacy with traditional learning, resulting in a dramatic shift in standardized test scores.

Rural Electrification

By establishing decentralized solar micro-grids, we bypassed failing central infrastructure. This project not only provided reliable energy but also catalyzed local micro-economies and extended operating hours for medical clinics.
