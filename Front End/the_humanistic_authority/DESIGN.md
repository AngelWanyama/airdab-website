## Brand & Style

This design system is built for a modern, mission-driven organization that balances institutional credibility with a warm, humanistic touch. The aesthetic rejects the coldness of traditional corporate design in favor of an "Experimental Classical" style. By flipping the traditional dark mode hierarchy—utilizing an Amber-led accent structure against deep, authoritative backgrounds—we create a provocative yet polished digital experience.

The visual language draws from **High-Contrast Minimalism** and **Editorial Design**. It prioritizes extreme typographic clarity, purposeful use of negative space, and a bespoke layout feel that mirrors high-end print journalism. The goal is to evoke a sense of "Urgent Wisdom"—the feeling that the organization is both established and actively working toward a better future.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a deep foundation, now significantly brightened in its neutral tones to enhance readability and professional "sheen." This color strategy moves away from muddy darks toward a high-clarity, luminous dark mode.

- **Primary (Amber - #f59e0b):** The emotional heart of the system. Used for critical calls to action, active states, and high-impact human-centric elements. It represents the "mission" and "energy."
- **Secondary (Pure White - #ffffff):** A stark, brilliant contrast used for primary text and key structural highlights. It provides clarity and a modern "editorial" sharpness.
- **Tertiary (Soft Slate - #64748b):** Utilized for secondary text, borders, and subtle UI metadata, providing a contemporary bridge between the dark backgrounds and bright accents.
- **Surface Strategy:** We use a neutral foundation (#e9e9e9) to derive our surfaces. This ensures that even in dark mode, elevated containers and cards have a crisp, clean architectural quality that avoids unwanted color casts.

## Typography

The typographic strategy is the cornerstone of this design system's "Humanistic Authority." 

**EB Garamond** is employed for all editorial headlines. It should be set with tight tracking in larger sizes to emphasize its classical elegance. Its organic, slightly irregular forms provide a necessary human contrast to the digital environment.

**Manrope** serves as the functional workhorse. Its geometric yet approachable structure ensures high legibility for data-heavy sections and body copy. For labels and "overlines," use Manrope in uppercase with generous letter spacing to create a sense of organized, institutional rigor. 

For mobile devices, display sizes are aggressively scaled down to maintain readability while preserving the high-contrast relationship between headlines and body text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is based on a **12-column fixed grid** for desktop, prioritizing "generous whitespace" to allow complex mission-driven content to be easily digested. 

- **Vertical Rhythm:** A strict 8px baseline grid is used. Section headers should be preceded by significant "breathing room" (120px on desktop) to signal a change in narrative focus.
- **The "Bespoke" Grid:** Avoid centered, symmetrical layouts. Use asymmetrical column spans (e.g., text spanning 5 columns with a 7-column image offset) to create an editorial, premium feel.
- **Mobile Adaptation:** On mobile, the grid collapses to a single column with 20px side margins. Padding within cards and containers should remain generous to maintain the "polished" brand feel.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a "Contemporary but Serious" vibe, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows. Depth is instead conveyed through **Tonal Layering** and **Subtle Outlines**.

- **Surface Tiers:** The base background is the primary dark tone. Content cards and containers use slightly lighter tones derived from the neutral #e9e9e9 seed to create structural separation.
- **Borders:** Instead of shadows, use 1px borders in a low-contrast Soft Slate (#64748b) or semi-transparent secondary white. This creates a "blueprint" or "structured" feel that implies precision.
- **Glassmorphism (Contextual):** Use backdrop blurs (20px) on navigation bars and modal overlays to maintain a sense of place and transparency, reinforcing the "Human" aspect of the brand.

## Shapes

The shape language is defined by a **Medium Roundedness** (Level 2). This 0.5rem (8px) base radius is the "Golden Mean" for this system—it is soft enough to feel approachable and human, but sharp enough to maintain a professional, institutional structure.

- **Primary Components:** Buttons and input fields use the standard 8px radius.
- **Large Containers:** Image carousels and large section cards may use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to create a softer, more inviting frame for high-impact photography.
- **Icons:** Use linear, 2px stroke icons with slightly rounded terminals to match the typography's weight.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Amber (#f59e0b) with dark text. No shadow. The contrast should be jarring and immediate.
- **Secondary:** Outlined White (#ffffff) with a 1px border. On hover, the border and text transition to Amber.
- **Tertiary:** Text-only with an Amber underline that expands on hover.

### Cards & Program Layouts
- Cards should feature a "Subtle Overlay" technique: high-impact photography with a neutral-to-transparent gradient at the bottom to ensure EB Garamond headlines remain legible over images.
- Use generous internal padding (32px+) to maintain the premium feel.

### Input Fields
- Dark backgrounds with a 1px Slate or Neutral border. On focus, the border shifts to Amber and the label (Manrope Bold) floats above the field in a reduced size, set in White for maximum legibility.

### Imagery
- Photography should be "Mission-Driven": authentic, high-resolution photos of people and environments. Avoid generic stock. Apply a very slight desaturation to align photography with the Slate and Neutral palette.

### Navigation
- A fixed header with a high-degree backdrop blur. The logo should be positioned on the left, with uppercase Manrope links on the right in White, providing a clear, institutional navigation path.
